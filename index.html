BEGINNER GUIDE

How to Play

Build your farm step by step and turn your harvest into progress.

🌱1. Plant

Choose a seed and place it on an empty farm plot.

💧2. Water

Water your crops so they can continue their growth cycle.

🌿3. Grow

Watch the crop progress through its growth stages.

🧺4. Harvest

Harvest crops when they are ready to collect their value.

🪙5. Earn

Turn harvested crops into coins for your farm.

🛒6. Upgrade

Buy more seeds and improve your farm as you progress.

DATA STRUCTURES LAB

DSA Lab

Explore how each data structure solves a specific problem in GardenSim.

🟩Python OOP

Plant, Garden, Stack, Queue, TreeNode, and game structures are modeled as Python classes.

▦2D List

The farm grid uses rows and columns to represent garden plots.

📚Stack

Action history follows LIFO so the latest action can be removed first.

⏳Queue

Weather events follow FIFO so the first event is processed first.

🌳Tree

Plant categories are organized hierarchically and can be traversed.

🔎Binary Search Tree

Stores the crop catalog in sorted order for efficient searching.

🗺️Graph

Models connected farm locations and finds a route between them.

Implementation Notes

Python OOP

Plant, Garden, ActionHistoryStack, WeatherQueue, TreeNode, and PlantHierarchy are Python classes.

Stack

append() acts as push and pop() removes the newest action. Typical end operations are O(1).

Queue

A list with a front index is used for FIFO behavior without repeatedly shifting the whole list.

Hierarchical Tree

Plants are organized as Plants → Vegetables/Fruits → subcategories → plant names.
